Manufacturing and Quality Process

A clear, practical walk-through of how raw cotton is cleaned, scoured, bleached, carded, rolled with interleaving paper, packed, and verified at our Ratwai unit in Gwalior.

Step-by-Step Procedure

How Our Cotton Is Processed

Clean handling, honest weight checks, and direct floor supervision ensure uniform absorbent cotton rolls and dependable cotton wicks.

1

Cotton Selection & Cleaning

Raw cotton fibers are selected and mechanically opened to eliminate seeds, husks, and leafy plant particles. Clean, healthy staple fibers are separated to ensure softness and prevent harsh grit in the finished roll.

2

Scouring & Bleaching (High Absorbency)

The natural waxy and oily coating of natural cotton prevents it from soaking up water. During scouring, these waxes are removed. The fibers are then bleached in clean wash tanks to achieve a bright optical white color, neutralizing any acidity and yielding high capillary absorbency suitable for clinical and surgical applications.

3

Drying & Carding

After thorough washing, the bleached cotton is dried and passed through carding machines. The carding cylinders gently comb the fibers into smooth, continuous parallel webs with uniform density, ensuring the roll has consistent thickness with no hard lumps or thin patches.

4

Interleaving Paper & Rolling

For our surgical cotton rolls, the combed sheet is rolled together with a clean, medical-grade blue interleaving paper. This interleaving layer prevents the cotton sheet from sticking to itself, allowing doctors, nurses, and compounders to unroll and cut the exact length required easily and hygienically.

5

Cotton Wick Twisting & Bundling

For cotton wicks (diya batti), clean bleached fibers are shaped into firm, uniform wicks. The balanced twist allows the wick to absorb oil or ghee steadily, burning with a calm, smokeless flame for domestic puja, temple lamps, and festive lighting.

6

Accurate Weighing & Quality Verification

Our supervisor directly monitors the weighing scales. Net Weight packs (500g, 80g, 40g, 20g) are verified so that buyers receive full cotton weight. For Gross Weight packs (80g and 40g), the wrapping allowance is explicitly declared to ensure transparent dealing with wholesalers and retailers.

7

Protective Packing & Dispatch Ready

Each surgical roll is wrapped in protective packaging to prevent dust or moisture ingress during transit. Cotton wicks are sealed into clear consumer packets and packed in bundles of 6 or 12 pieces per packet. Finished master cartons and bags are staged for local road transport across Madhya Pradesh and neighboring states.

Direct Quality Accountability

Because we operate a hands-on unit with our supervisor on the floor alongside our 10 dedicated daily wage workers, any batch variation is immediately flagged and corrected. You get reliable, consistent rolls and wicks in every single shipment.
