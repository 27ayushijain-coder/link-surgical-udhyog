About Link Surgical Udhyog

A hands-on cotton manufacturing firm founded in 2020 in Ratwai Village, Gwalior district, Madhya Pradesh. Grounded in direct dealing, honest weighing, and dependable supply.

Link Surgical Udhyog was established in 2020 in Ratwai Village, Gwalior district (Madhya Pradesh) as a sole proprietorship cotton manufacturing enterprise. The unit was founded with a straightforward goal: to manufacture clean, honest-weight absorbent surgical cotton rolls and cotton wicks, supplying business buyers directly from the factory floor without intermediaries or inflated retail margins.

The Ratwai Manufacturing Unit

Our unit is situated in the rural industrial vicinity of Ratwai Village in Gwalior district. Being locally situated with direct connectivity to Gwalior city, the Chambal region, Morena, Bhind, Shivpuri, Datia, and adjoining routes of Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, we are able to provide prompt and cost-effective dispatch of bulk cartons and bags.

Our Hands-On Working Philosophy

Unlike large corporate conglomerates or commercial trade brokers, we operate as a tight, hands-on manufacturing unit. Every order is directly known to our proprietor and supervisor. When you place an enquiry, you speak directly with the people who manage the production line.

We do not make exaggerated claims of massive capacities or unverified certifications. Our reputation is built on straightforward, reliable practices: honest declarations of Net Weight versus Gross Weight, clean handling of pure white bleached cotton fibers, dependable packing, and fair ex-factory wholesale prices.
